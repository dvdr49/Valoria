About Valoria

Learn about the identity, structure and digital community that make up Valoria.

A Micronational Project

The State of Valoria is a micronational project and online community built around a shared identity, symbols, institutions and community activities.

Valoria maintains its own organizational structure and civic framework for members participating in the project.

The official website serves as the central digital point for information, announcements, institutions and community activities.

Government of Valoria

Federal Government

The Federal Government of Valoria is organized into executive, legislative and judicial branches, supported by federal departments serving the Valorian community.

E
Executive Branch

Presidency

The executive branch is led by the President and Vice President, with the Cabinet supporting the administration of the Federal Government.

L
Legislative Branch

Congress

The legislative branch consists of the Senate and the House of Representatives and provides a framework for community representation.

J
Judicial Branch

Supreme Court

The judicial branch provides interpretation and review within Valoria's internal civic and constitutional framework.

Federal Departments

Each department has a defined role within Valoria's digital community and federal administration.

01 DOS

Department of State

Responsible for Valoria's external relations, diplomatic communication and relationships with other micronational projects and communities.

  • External relations
  • Diplomatic communications
  • Micronational partnerships
  • Official representatives
02 DOC

Department of Communications

Responsible for official communications, digital media, announcements and Valoria's public information channels.

  • Official announcements
  • Website communications
  • Social media
  • Public information
03 DHS

Department of Homeland Security

Responsible for internal digital security, community safety and protection of official online spaces.

  • Digital community security
  • Protection of official platforms
  • Community safety
  • Security coordination
04 DOJ

Department of Justice

Responsible for internal legal administration, rules, complaints and matters concerning Valoria's civic framework.

  • Internal rules
  • Complaints and disputes
  • Civic procedures
  • Justice administration
